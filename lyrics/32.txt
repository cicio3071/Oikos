Symbiosis

Come down where the mosses do not ask your name
走入那個苔蘚不詢問你名字的地方
where roots have long since swallowed stone and sky
樹根早已將石頭與天空一同吞噬
I've been waiting in the language of the rain
我用雨的語言在這裡等候
in everything that bends instead of breaking wide
在所有彎折而非折斷的事物之中

You fought the mountain like it owed you ground
你曾與山抗爭，彷彿它欠你立足之地
but nothing here is won—it's only given
但這裡沒有什麼是赢得的，只有給予
So let your weight fall where I make the sound
讓你的重量落在我所在的地方
and learn the way the living things are driven
學習，那些活著的事物被什麼驅動

We are Symbiosis
我們是共生
blood and bark and bone
血液、樹皮與骨骼
the valley holds our voices
溪谷保存著我們的聲音
in frequencies unknown
在那些無名的頻率裡
Not a conquest, not a surrender
不是征服，也不是投降
just the deepest kind of known
只是，最深處的彼此了解

The orchids bore witness from the wall of stone
石斛從岩壁上默默見證
the camphor spread its arms across our heat
樟樹張開了手臂，覆蓋我們的熱
the water carried what we could not own
河水承載著我們無法擁有的事
and buried it somewhere beneath our feet
埋入了，我們腳下更深的地方
