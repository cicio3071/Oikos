The Melted Sunset

The gold is dripping down the skyline line
金色的光芒正沿著天際線流淌
Bleeding through the clouds, a vintage wine
滲透雲層，宛如一抹陳年的酒紅
The shadows stretch their fingers long and thin
影子伸出纖細而漫長的手指
To catch the fire before the night begins
想在黑夜降臨前，接住最後的餘火
We’re standing where the ocean meets the haze
我們佇立在海天煙嵐的交界
Lost inside this amber-colored maze
迷失在這座琥珀色的迷宮裡

The warmth is leaving, but the glow remains
溫暖正漸漸抽離，但那份餘輝依然閃耀
Flowing like a fever through our veins
像一場高燒，在我們的血管裡奔流
Everything we knew is turning blurred
我們所熟悉的一切正變得模糊
The quietest goodbye I’ve ever heard
這是我聽過，最寂靜的一次告別

Oh, the melted sunset’s pouring in the sea
噢，融化的落日正傾瀉入海
Drowning all the ghosts of you and me
淹沒了那些屬於你我的往事幽靈
Yellow turns to purple, then to gray
金黃轉為深紫，最終歸於黯淡的灰
As the heavy daylight slips away
隨著沉重的白晝悄然溜走
Keep the colors locked inside your eyes
請將這些色彩鎖在你的眼底
Before the burning embers start to die
在燃燒的餘燼熄滅之前
(Melted sunset... drifting in the tide)
(融化的落日……在潮汐中漂流）

The city lights are waking one by one
城市的燈火正一盞盞甦醒
Diamonds scattered where there was a sun
在太陽曾駐足的地方，散落著鑽石般的碎光
The asphalt’s cooling under neon signs
柏油路面在霓虹招牌下逐漸冷卻
But I’m still tracing all the orange lines
但我仍固執地，追尋著那些橘色的殘影
Remembering the way you used to glow
記得你曾經發光的模樣
Before the gravity began to pull us low
在現實的重力將我們向下拉扯之前

It’s not an ending, just a change of light
這並非終結，只是光影的轉換
A beautiful surrender to the night
一場對黑夜最絕美的臣服
Hold my hand until the world goes black
握住我的手，直到世界沒入黑暗
There’s no use in trying to call the daylight back
試著喚回白晝已徒勞無功

Oh, the melted sunset’s pouring in the sea
噢，融化的落日正傾瀉入海
Drowning all the ghosts of you and me

請將這些色彩鎖在你的眼底

在燃燒的餘燼熄滅之前

(消融殆盡……）

(沒入虛無……）
The stars are hungry for the afterglow
繁星正渴求著最後的餘暉
Just let it go, just let it go
就隨它去吧，就讓它走吧
