The Second Skin of Stone

Step by step on the giant’s stairs
一步又一步，踏上巨人的階梯。  
Thin air breathes through our tangled hair
稀薄的空氣，拂過我們交纏的髮絲。  
You touch the wall, the gray Andes bone
妳觸摸著石牆，那灰色的安地斯之骨。  
Seeking the secrets the Incas have known
尋覓著印加人曾知曉的祕密。  
No mortar, no clay, just a perfect fit
沒有灰泥，沒有黏土，只有完美的契合。  
In the shadow of Inti, where the ages sit
在太陽神的陰影下，歲月靜靜坐落。  

The warrior stands at the edge of the deep
戰士矗立在深淵的邊緣。  
While the Sun God watches the promises we keep
當太陽神注視著我們信守的承諾。  
This stone isn’t cold, it’s a heartbeat, a shield
這石頭並不冰冷，它是心跳，也是護盾。  
To the fires of the past, it would never yield
面對過去的戰火，它永不屈服。  

Oh, call it a revolutionary friend
噢，稱之為有著「革命情誼」的戰友。  
A love that remains when the empires end
這份愛，在帝國覆滅後依然長存。  
You are my anchor, the skin of my soul
妳是我的錨點，是我靈魂的肌膚。  
In this hollow of time, you make me whole
在時間的夾縫中，妳使我完整。  
Henosis in the dust, Henosis in the light
塵埃中的結合，光芒裡的結合。  
The stones are our witness in the fading night
石頭是我們在消逝黑夜裡的見證。  

Wrapped in the blue of the Condor’s wing
裹在神鷹之翼的湛藍之中。  
Small cups of coca, the comfort they bring
小杯的古柯茶，帶來了些許慰藉。  
You feed me the harvest, the Earth’s ancient grace
你餵我品嚐收成，那大地古老的恩典。  
The scent of the soil on your beautiful face
你美麗臉龐上，帶著泥土的芬芳。  
No need to change, no need to renew
無需改變，也無需更新。  
Existence is enough when I’m standing with you
當我與你並肩，存在本身已足夠。  

We learn from the steps, we learn from the height
我們向階梯學習，向高度學習。  
To watch and to listen, to stay in the light
去觀看，去傾聽，停留在光芒之中。  
Like the water that flows in the Paqcha's old vein
如同流淌在古老水道脈絡裡的水。  
Our story's a mountain, immune to the rain
我們的故事是一座山，不懼風雨。  

Oh, call it a revolutionary friend
噢，稱之為有著「革命情誼」的戰友。  
A love that remains when the empires end
這份愛，在帝國覆滅後依然長存。  
You are my anchor, the skin of my soul
妳是我的錨點，是我靈魂的肌膚。  
In this hollow of time, you make me whole
在時間的夾縫中，妳使我完整。  
Henosis in the dust, Henosis in the light
塵埃中的結合，光芒裡的結合。  
The stones are our witness in the fading night
石頭是我們在消逝黑夜裡的見證。

Just stay like the wall...
就如石牆般停留...  
Just breathe like the hill...
就如山丘般呼吸...  
In the silence of ruins, I love you still.
在遺跡的靜默中，我依然愛妳。
