Crimson Guidance

Under the canopy where the crimson rains fall
在那片深紅花雨落下的穹頂之下 
The ancient cherry tree stands guard over all
古老的櫻花樹靜默地守護著這一切 
A hunter was lost in the mountain's cold vein
獵人在山的冷冽脈絡中迷失了方向 
Searching for a shore through the fog and the pain
在迷霧與痛楚中尋找著停靠的岸 


The fox broke its throat just to light up the way
狐狸咬開了咽喉，只為將歸途點亮 
Crimson lanterns blooming, turning night into day
深紅的燈籠綻放，讓黑夜化作白晝光芒 
Now you are the boat and I am the shore
如今你是那艘船，而我是你的彼岸 
In the nest of the loft, we wander no more
在閣樓的巢穴裡，我們不再四處流浪 


An old rusted trap sleeping deep in the ground
一具生鏽的捕獸夾，深眠在泥土的懷抱 
Healing the scars with the flowers we found
用我們尋得的花朵，將往日的傷痕醫好 
Time is a circle, not a line running thin
時間是個圓，而非那條追逐著我們的直線 
Stay in the Oikos, let the healing begin
留在這歸屬之地，讓療癒在當下蔓延 


No more cold violence, no more ghosts in the mist
不再有冷暴力的陰影，迷霧中的幽靈已散去 
Just the breath of the mountain, in a starlight kiss
只有山的呼吸，在星光下的親吻裡傳遞 


The boat has found its anchor in the deep
這艘船已在深處找到了它的錨點 
Close your eyes, my love, let the spirits sleep
閉上雙眼吧，我的愛人，讓山靈安然入眠 
We are home... we are home...
我們到家了...我們到家了...
