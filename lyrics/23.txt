Selenê Aimatôdês

The hearth fire flickers, a ghost on the wall
爐火明滅閃爍，如牆上的幽影
While the sky prepares for its crimson fall
而天空正醞釀著一場緋紅的墜落
You ask of the nectar, the golden dried bloom
你問起那份花蜜，那金色的乾花
It’s the scent of the stars in this small wooden room
那是這小小木屋裡，星辰的香氣 
The Blue and the Violet, they scatter and fade
藍與紫的波光紛紛散射凋零 
Leaving the shadows that we have both made
留下我們共同依偎的暗影


Selenê turns red in the velvet height
月神塞勒涅在絲絨般的高空轉為緋紅 
Two souls merging in the alchemy of light
兩個靈魂在光的煉金術中交融 
It’s not a darkness, but a fevered glow
那並非黑暗，而是灼熱的微光
Where the deep tides pull and the wild winds blow
在深潮湧動、狂風吹拂的地方 
In this Henosis, we finally see
在這靈魂的合一中，我們終於看見 
The blood-colored light of our destiny
那道血色的命運之光


The Earth is a filter, a veil in the dark
地球化作濾鏡，黑暗中的薄紗 
Igniting the moon with a silent red spark
以無聲的紅火點燃了月亮
You’re leaning against me, a anchor so still
你倚靠著我，是如此靜謐的錨 
Against the vast curve of the cosmic will
對抗著宇宙意志那浩瀚的弧線
Forget the old stories of warnings and fear
忘卻那些關於警告與恐懼的舊傳說 
The only rebirth is the one we find here
在此地，我們找到了唯一的重生

No maps for this voyage, no lines on the page
這場航行沒有地圖，書頁上沒有字跡 
Just the heat of your pulse, a soul out of cage
只有你脈搏的溫熱，那破籠而出的靈魂
The world disappears in a crimson embrace
世界消逝在緋紅的擁抱裡
As we drift through the silence of infinite space
當我們漂流過無限空間的寂靜


The moon starts to bleed into the dark of the sea
月亮開始滲入深海的黑暗
Tonight, you are the only eclipse I see
今晚，你是我眼中唯一的蝕缺
