Our Silent Myth

Shadows dancing on the cavern walls
影子在岩壁上翩翩起舞。  
Winter wind, the ocean calls
冬季的風，海洋在呼喚。  
We buried the ghosts in the sand below
我們將幽靈埋在腳下的沙土裡。  
In the warmth of the ember, we let them go
在餘燼的溫暖中，我們讓它們隨風而去。  
You took my hand, through the salt and the spray
你牽著我的手，穿過鹽粒與浪花。  
Turned the ghosts of the past into yesterday
將過去的幽靈化作昨日的塵煙。  

Oh, look into the heart of the beast
噢，望向那海怪的心臟。  
Where the fire and the stones bring us peace
那裡的火光與岩石帶給我們平靜。  
You’re the anchor, I’m the light
你是錨點，我是光。  
Reborn in the hollow of a Christmas night
在聖誕夜的岩洞中重生。  
No more turning back, no more "why"
不再回頭，不再追問「為什麼」。  
Just the myth we create, you and I
只有你我共同創造的神話。  

A linear line in a world so deep
在這深邃世界裡的一條直線。  
Promises made while the seagulls sleep
在海鷗沉睡時許下的諾言。  
The scent of coffee, the weight of your palm
咖啡的香氣，你手掌的重量。  
You’re the storm’s end, you’re my calm
你是風暴的終點，你是我的寧靜。  
Like a wild bird flying to the southern sun
就像一隻飛向南方陽光的野鳥。  
The long winter’s over, our life has begun
漫長的冬季已結束，我們的生活才剛開始。  

Sakhi, my soul, my friend, my kin
Sakhi，我的靈魂，我的摯友，我的親人。  
Where the ocean ends, our stories begin
海洋結束的地方，是我們故事的起點。  
Not just a teammate, not just a ghost
不僅僅是隊友，不僅僅是幻影。  
But the home that I love the most
而是我最深愛的家。  
Every breath is a window, every window a view
每一次呼吸都是一扇窗，每一扇窗都是一幅風景。  
Of a world that is healing, because of you
關於這正在痊癒的世界，全是因為你。  

So look into the heart of the beast
所以望向那海怪的心臟。

Always home.
永遠是家。
