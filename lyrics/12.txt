Henosis in the Sacred Breath

Golden light on the mud-brick walls
金色陽光灑在泥磚牆上  
Urubamba whispers as the evening falls
當夜幕降臨，烏魯班巴河低語  
Thin air, warm tea, a sacred grace
稀薄的空氣、溫暖的茶，一份神聖的恩典  
I find my home within your embrace
在你的擁抱中，我找到了歸宿  
From the ocean’s edge to the mountain’s height
從海洋之際到山脈之巔  
We left the noise for the ancient light
我們離開喧囂，奔向古老的光芒  

No maps needed, no stars to chase
無需地圖，亦無需追逐繁星  
Just the pulse of the earth, and your steady pace
只有大地的脈動，與你穩健的步調  
Pachamama’s breath in the eucalyptus breeze
大地母親的氣息隨尤加利微風吹拂  
We’re finding our rhythm among the trees
我們在林間找尋彼此的節奏  

Henosis, our secret name
Henosis，我們秘密的名字  
Two different sparks, but a single flame
兩簇不同的火花，燃成同一個火焰  
It’s the river’s flow, it’s the mountain’s soul
它是河流的奔騰，是山脈的靈魂  
In the "Gentle Valley," we are whole
在這「溫柔之谷」，我們完整合一  
Añay to the earth, Añay to the blue
感謝大地，感謝那片蔚藍  
Every step I take, I’m coming home to you
我踏出的每一步，都是在向你歸家  

Market colors and ancient threads
市集的色彩與古老的絲線  
Stories woven where the spirits tread
在靈魂踏足之地織就的故事  
Purple corn and the scent of the soil
紫色玉米與土地的芬芳  
Love is the rest after all the toil
愛是所有辛勞後的棲息  
You are my anchor, I am your sight
你是我的錨點，我是你的眼界  
Cradled by the Andes in the soft twilight
在安地斯山脈柔和的暮光中被輕輕環抱  

Not a label, but a verb we live
不是一個標籤，而是我們實踐的動詞  
Every breath we take, every heart we give
我們的每一次呼吸，獻出的每一顆心  
J-City’s tides have turned to stone
J城的潮汐已化為堅石  
In this Sacred Valley, we are never alone
在這片聖谷，我們永不孤單  

Henosis, our secret name
Henosis，我們秘密的名字  
Two different sparks, but a single flame
兩簇不同的火花，燃成同一個火焰  
It’s the river’s flow, it’s the mountain’s soul
它是河流的奔騰，是山脈的靈魂  
In the "Gentle Valley," we are whole
在這「溫柔之谷」，我們完整合一  
Añay to the earth, Añay to the blue
感謝大地，感謝那片蔚藍  
Every step I take, I’m coming home to you
我踏出的每一步，都是在向你歸家  

"Ἐν τῇ ψυχῇ μου ἔχεις," you softly say
「你在我的靈魂裡」，你溫柔地低語  
As the Milky Way lights our ancient way
當銀河照亮我們古老的道路  
Just you and me...
只有你和我  
In the breath of the Andes...
在安地斯山脈的呼吸中...
