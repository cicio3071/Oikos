The Great Dream

Before the names, before the separating skin
在名字之前，在皮膚將我們分隔之前
we floated in a cloud and shared the same dream
我們漂浮在雲裡，共做著同一場夢
no hunger and no edges, no beginning
沒有飢餓，沒有邊界，沒有起點
and nothing lost because there was no seam
沒有失去，因為還沒有縫隙


But something pulled us downward, one by one
但某種力量把我們一個一個往下拉
we landed hard on ground that didn't give
我們重重地落在不讓步的土地上
and stood there in the silence, stunned
站在那裡，被沉默包圍，茫然失措
not knowing what it meant to separately live
不知道，分開地活著，是什麼意思


We looked at one another through the fog
我們透過霧氣，彼此凝視
and saw the shape of someone we once knew
看見了某個我們曾經認識的輪廓
but all the shared language had dissolved
但所有共同的語言都已消融
and what remained was only — me and you
剩下的，只有我和你


So someone drew a hand upon the wall
於是有人在牆上畫下一隻手
and someone sharpened stone to say I'm here
有人磨利了石頭，說我在這裡
and someone kept the fire burning through the dark
有人讓火在黑暗中燃燒整夜
not to be seen — just to make the dark less clear
不是為了被看見，只是為了讓黑暗不那麼絕對
this is how we learned to speak again
這就是我們重新學會說話的方式
across the silence, across the distance and the rain
穿越沉默，穿越距離與雨


You said you weren't the one who chose to fall
你說你不是那個選擇墜落的人
that you were thrown before you found your ground
你被推出去，還沒找到自己的落腳處
so you became the shape the world expected
於是你變成了世界期待的形狀
and hid the softer faces that you'd found
把所有更柔軟的面貌，都藏了起來


But hiding is a kind of cartography
但藏匿，也是一種製圖的方式
it maps the self that lives beneath the skin
它描繪著那個，活在皮膚之下的自我
I wasn't searching — I was in the same fog
我不是在尋找，我也在同一片霧裡
two exiles, learning where the other's been
兩個流亡者，學著了解對方走過的路


So someone drew a hand upon the wall
於是有人在牆上畫下一隻手
and someone sharpened stone to say I'm here
有人磨利了石頭，說我在這裡
and someone kept the fire burning through the dark
有人讓火在黑暗中燃燒整夜
not to be seen — just to make the dark less clear
不是為了被看見，只是為了讓黑暗不那麼絕對
this is how we learned to speak again
這就是我們重新學會說話的方式
across the silence, across the distance and the rain
穿越沉默，穿越距離與雨


We don't need the same dream anymore
我們不再需要同一場夢
we only need the same fire
我們只需要同一團火
not to dissolve back into cloud
不是為了重新融入雲裡
but to stand on ground together
而是為了，一起站在土地上
two people who were pushed into the wild
兩個被推進荒野的人
who stopped looking for the dream
停止尋找那場夢
and started tending to each other's fire
開始，照料彼此的火


So I drew a hand upon the wall for you
於是我為你，在牆上畫下一隻手
and sharpened everything I had to say I'm here
磨利了我所有想說的，說我在這裡
and kept the fire burning through your dark
讓火在你的黑暗中燃燒
not to be seen — just so you'd know I'm near
不是為了被看見，只是讓你知道，我在旁邊
this is how we learned to find each other
這就是我們找到彼此的方式
two dreamers who fell separately
兩個分開墜落的夢遊者
and landed — together
落地，在一起


before the names, we shared the same dream
在名字之前，我們共做同一場夢
now we share the same fire
現在，我們共守同一團火
and that is enough
這就夠了
