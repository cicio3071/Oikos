The Scars of the Sun

Two stones resting in a velvet palm
兩顆石頭靜臥在柔軟的手心  
One hitches the sun, one keeps the life calm
一顆拴住了太陽，一顆守護著生活的平靜  
The dust of Cusco still on your hair
庫斯科的塵土仍停留在妳的髮梢  
We’re breathing in the thin, high-altitude air
我們正呼吸著稀薄的高原空氣  
You asked me why the ruins have to fall
妳問我，為何那些遺跡註定要崩塌  
I told you the heart is the greatest museum of all
我告訴妳，心靈才是這世上最偉大的博物館  
From the fisherman’s net to the curator’s light
從漁夫的漁網到館長的光芒
We’re weaving a thread through the Andean night
我們正編織著一條穿越安地斯之夜的線  

Oh, it’s the itch of the healing, the silver of the scar
哦，那是癒合時的搔癢，是傷疤流動的銀光  
We’re chasing the sunrise, no matter how far
無論多遠，我們都在追逐著日出  
Between the phantom pain and the anchor we found
在曾經的幻痛與我們尋得的錨點之間  
Our love is the only holy ground
我們的愛是唯一的聖地
ἕνωσις —where the rivers meet the sea
ἕνωσις —那是河流匯入大海之處  
You are the lighthouse, the island for me
妳是我的燈塔，是我停靠的島嶼  

High above the clouds, thirty thousand feet
在雲層之上，三萬英尺的高空  
Where the past and the future quietly meet
過去與未來在那裡靜靜相遇  
A paper bird folded from a stranger’s line
用陌生人的字條摺成的一隻紙鳥  
But your eyes are the only stars I define
但妳的雙眼，才是我定義的唯一星辰  
You whispered of shadows, of ghosts in the book
妳低語著陰影，低語著書中的幽靈  
I promised to be the only way you look
我承諾，我會成為妳唯一的注視方向  
The scab is a shield, let the new skin grow
結痂是妳的護盾，讓新生的肌膚生長  
We’re leaving the hollows for the warmth we know
