The Orchid and the Anchor

I followed footsteps pressed in unfamiliar stone
我跟著他的腳印走，踩在陌生的石頭上
through fog that hadn't learned to part for me
穿越那些還不習慣為我讓路的霧
I gripped the belt and tried to hold my own
我緊抓著腰帶，試圖保持重心
but the mountain doesn't yield to who needs
但山從不對那些需要它的人讓步

He said the stones don't like to be subdued
他說，石頭不喜歡被征服
they only open up for those who hear
它們只對那些願意傾聽的人敞開
I breathed and let the valley be my guide
我深呼吸，讓溪谷成為我的嚮導
and found I'd been arriving, all this while
才發現，我一直正在抵達

The orchid blooms where no one planted it
石斛花開在從未有人播種的地方
still something tends to it, the rain, the dark
但總有什麼在照料它，雨，和黑暗
I am the anchor's weight and where it sits
我是那錨的重量，也是它沉落的地方
and he's the current that has learned my heart
而他是那股水流，早已熟記我心跳的節拍

They were watching—the stream, the ancient bark
它們都在看著，溪流、古老的樹皮
the petals holding light between their seams
花瓣之間，夾藏著穿透而來的光
not as strangers to our open dark
不是作為陌生者，見證我們的敞開
but as the oldest keepers of such things
而是作為，見證這類事物最古老的守護者

The orchid blooms where no one planted it
石斛花開在從未有人播種的地方
still something tends to it, the rain, the dark
但總有什麼在照料它，雨，和黑暗
I am the anchor's weight and where it sits
我是那錨的重量，也是它沉落的地方
and he's the current that has learned my heart
而他是那股水流，早已熟記我心跳的節拍

We left no mark the valley hadn't kept
我們沒有留下任何溪谷未曾保留的痕跡
and still—it holds us, even as we slept
但它依然保存著我們，即使在我們沉睡之後
