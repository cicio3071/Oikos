The Shape of Verbs

Passing through the boundary, the narrow rocky gate
穿越這道邊界，那座狹窄的岩石門戶
The world behind us fades away, leaving only fate
背後的世界漸漸遠去，只剩下命運
Emerald water, shadows deep, a silent theater stage
翠綠的海水，深邃的陰影，如同一座寂靜的劇院舞台
"Glug glug" goes the ocean, turning a brand new page
大海發出「咕嚕咕嚕」的聲音，翻開了嶄新的一頁
I’m a little stone, polished by the tide
我是一顆小石頭，被潮汐洗鍊打磨
In your steady arms, I no longer have to hide.
在你穩固的懷抱中，我不再需要躲藏  

I’m the Great Whale, wandering this blue
我是那頭大鯨魚，在這片湛藍中徜徉
But my only compass now is leading back to you
但我現在唯一的羅盤，正指引我回到你身邊
Strip away the labels, the names the world provides
剝除那些標籤，剝除世界賦予的名字
Here within the nest, where the ancient spirit resides
在這座巢穴之中，古老的靈魂安居於此  

We are the verbs, moving through the deep
我們是動詞，在深處游動
Reject the nouns, and the promises they keep
拒絕名詞，以及它們所守護的承諾
To protect, to accompany, to explore the unknown
去守護、去陪伴、去探索未知
To float, to feel, to bloom where seeds are sown
去漂浮、去感受、在種子播下之處綻放
A cosmic ritual in this hidden sanctuary
在這座隱秘聖域中，舉行一場宇宙的儀式
Just you and me, becoming the extraordinary.
只有你和我，正變得與眾不同  

You are my tree, and I am the bird
你是我的樹，而我是那隻鳥
I’m the steady wave, and you’re the pearl unheard
我是穩定的浪，而你是那顆不曾被聽見的珍珠
Not an accident, but a destiny we chose
這不是一場意外，而是我們選擇的命運
As the starlight and the salt water compose.
當星光與鹽水共同譜寫樂章 

Just let the sun be our towel
就讓陽光當我們的毛巾
Let the wind dry our hair
讓微風吹乾我們的頭髮
In this cave of echoes...
在這座充滿回音的洞穴裡……
We are the verb...
我們就是動詞……
