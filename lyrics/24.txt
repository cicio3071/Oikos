The Curated Anchor: A Tale of J-Town

They say in a house where the cold firs grow
人們說，在那棟冷杉生長的木屋裡 
A wanderer rested with a world in tow
一位背負著整個世界的浪子停下了腳步 
With ink on his skin like a mountain’s face
他的皮膚刻滿如山巒起伏的墨跡 
He found his port in a quiet space
在那片靜謐之處，他找到了停靠的港灣 


The Curator watched with a silver gaze
策展人用他那如銀般的目光凝視 
Ignoring the signals and the digital haze

She traded her scrolls for the warmth of the pot
他用手中的卷軸，換取了那一鍋暖意

還有那份秋日饋贈的金花密語
