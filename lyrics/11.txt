The Shortest Spell

The pier of J-town smells of cedar and brine
J城的碼頭瀰漫著雪松與海水的鹹味。  
We’re not just carving wood, we’re carving time
我們不只是在雕刻木頭，而是在雕刻時光。  
You said the sea is a mirror of the soul
你說大海是靈魂的鏡子。  
And every broken piece can still be whole
每一塊碎片都能重獲完整。  
We watched the lanterns drift like fallen stars
我們看著水燈像墜落的星辰般漂流。  
Leaving the harbor, but keeping the scars.
離開了港口，卻留下了那些傷痕作為印記。  

One wing is a myth, or so they say
人們說，單翼的比翼鳥只是個神話。  
But two solo flights found the same way
但兩段獨自的飛行，找到了同一條路。  
Not for the lacks, nor the holes in the heart
並非為了填補殘缺，或心底的空洞。  
But for the map where the new travels start.
而是為了那張新旅程展開的地圖。  

One plus one is the magic of the sun
一加一，是太陽的魔法。  
Greater than two when the race is begun
當啟程之時，這份連結將大於二。  
A shortest spell, a name for the flight
最短的咒語，是這段飛行的名字。  
A flare for the storm, a signal for light
是風暴中的信號彈，是象徵光明的訊號。  
We’re not just an island, we’re the open sea
我們不再只是孤島，而是整片開放的海。  
You are the harbor, the journey, and me.
妳就是那港灣、那旅程，以及我。  

Linen on skin and bare feet on the floor
肌膚上的亞麻襯衫，與踩在地板上的赤腳。  
The fire is high behind the wooden door
木門後的爐火燒得正旺。  
The world wants a paper, a stamp, and a seal
這世界想要一張紙、一個印章與簽名。  
To verify what we already feel
去驗證我們早已感受到的心意。  
Not for the crowd, but for the "right" to stay
不為世人的認同，只為了那個留下來的「資格」。  
If the sky should fall, I won’t be turned away.
即使天塌下來，我也絕不離開。  

Tomorrow the Andes, the Apu of old
明天，我們前往安地斯山脈，在那古老山靈的身邊。 
Where the coca leaves speak of stories untold
在那裡，古柯葉訴說著未竟的故事。  
No signal, no wires, just the mountain’s breath
沒有訊號，沒有電線，只有山的呼吸。  
A covenant lived in life and in death
那是橫跨生死、鮮活的盟約。  
Always on the road, that’s where we reside
永遠在路上，那才是我們的棲身之所。  
With the fire of the hearth and the moon as our guide.
以壁爐的爐火與月光為導航。  

One plus one is the magic of the sun
一加一，是太陽的魔法。  
Greater than two when the race is begun
當啟程之時，這份連結將大於二。  
A shortest spell, a name for the flight
最短的咒語，是這段飛行的名字。  
A flare for the storm, a signal for light
是風暴中的信號彈，是象徵光明的訊號。  
We’re not just an island, we’re the open sea
我們不再只是孤島，而是整片開放的海。  
You are the harbor, the journey, and me.
你就是那港灣、那旅程，以及我。  

Wait for the rocking chair, wait for the grey
等待著搖椅，等待著白髮蒼蒼。  
But the road is the home we chose today
但道路就是我們今日選擇的家。  

The shortest spell...
那最短的咒語……
The Biyi’s flight...
那比翼的飛行……
