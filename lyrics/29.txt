The Overlapping Latitudes

The water was heavy, a mirror of grey
湖水曾如此沉重，映照出一片死灰
I was a fossil, forgotten by the day
我曾是一具化石，被白晝所遺忘
Until your current whispered, a low and steady song
直到你的洋流低聲歌唱，如此低沉而堅定
Pulling the silt where I’ve drifted so long
牽引著我那漂浮已久的淤泥


I mapped the stars just to watch them fall
我曾測繪星辰，卻只是看著它們墜落
A restless ghost, answering no one’s call
一個不安的幽靈，不回應任何人的呼喚
But in your silence, I found the line and the lead
但在你的沉默中，我找到了航線與牽引
A chart for the soul, a hunger to be fed
一張靈魂的圖表，一種對歸屬的渴望


We are the collision of two silent seas
我們是兩片寧靜深海的撞擊
The anchor is heavy, but the spirit is at ease
錨點雖重，但靈魂從此安穩
Henosis in the mist, where the paths overlap
在迷霧中合一，在那航道重疊之處
No more wandering, we’ve escaped the trap
不再四處流浪，我們已逃離了那場困境


You gave me the compass, the "Why" and the "When"
你給了我指南針，賦予了「為何」與「何時」意義 
You gave me the sunlight, making me whole again
你給了我陽光，讓我再次完整
