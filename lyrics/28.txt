The Boundless Gravity

The Andes whispered secrets through the frozen stone

We’ve traded the ghosts for a rhythm of our own

The condor’s wing is tracing paths we’ve yet to name

Two riverbeds converging, never twice the same
兩條河床匯流交織，從此再無重複的瞬間


My heartbeat is a compass calibrated by your tide

From the ruins of the Sun to the salt of J-town’s pier
從太陽神廟的斷垣，到 J 城碼頭的鹽分
The galaxy is a sketch that only we can hear
銀河是一幅素描，僅有你我能聽見其迴響


The voyage is circular, the horizon has no end
這場航行是圓周般的循環，地平線永無盡頭
Henosis is the sea where all the starlight will descend

Symbiosis, the breath between the peaks and the deep
「共生」，是山巔與深淵之間那道悠長的呼吸
A promise made in stardust that the ocean will keep
那是星塵立下的誓言，將由大海永恆守護
