The Reed and the Anchor

The reeds are singing where the mountain meets the tide
在山巒與潮汐相接之處，蘆葦正在歌唱 
Of a man with ink-stained skin and nowhere left to hide
訴說著一位皮膚佈滿墨跡、無處可藏的男人 
He spoke in rhythms older than the town’s grey stone
他的話語帶有比這座古鎮灰岩更古老的節奏 
And built an Oikos in the wood, no longer alone
他在林間築起了 Oikos，不再形單影隻 


The Curator of shadows, with starlight in her sleeves
影子的策展人，袖口沾滿了星光 
Found the truth of Aletheia among the fallen leaves
在落葉之間，尋獲了 Aletheia 的模樣 
She traded digital echoes for a bowl of golden blooms
她用數位的迴聲，換取了一碗金色的盛放 
Now the scent of sweet Osmanthus fills the attic rooms
如今，桂花的甜香盈滿了閣樓的空間 


Play the Aulos for the wanderer who found his rest
為那找到歸宿的浪子，吹響奧洛斯管吧
For the Curated Anchor, the heart inside the nest
為了那被悉心策展的錨點，那巢穴裡的核心 
Henosis in the twilight, a pact the red moon signed
在暮光中「合一」，那是紅月簽署的契約 
A love the bards will whisper, but never truly find
一段吟遊詩人會低聲傳頌，卻無人能真正尋獲的愛

Ἐν τῇ ψυχῇ μου ἔχεις... 
你在我的靈魂裡...
The footprints on the ridge are washing into grey
山脊上的足跡正逐漸化為一片蒼灰
For legends always linger as the lovers drift away
因為當戀人遠去時，唯有傳說會長久徘徊

A shell on an ankle, a name lost to the sea
腳踝上的貝殼，消逝在海裡的名字 
Just a song in the rafters of what used to be
只剩下這首歌，依舊在曾經的屋樑間迴盪
