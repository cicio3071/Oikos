Whispers of the Cansadas

Pink stones lying on the mountain side
粉紅色的岩石橫臥在山腰
Paused in time, with nowhere left to hide
時間在此停駐，無處遁逃 
You touched the cold rock, felt the ancient fear
妳觸碰冰冷的岩石，感受那古老的恐懼
Of a fallen empire, and the warrior's tear
關於隕落的帝國，以及戰士的淚水
"It's just a sadness," you whispered so low
「只是有點感傷，」妳低聲呢喃
But I saw the history in your eyes start to flow
但我看見歷史在妳眼中開始流淌   

Oh, my curator, your heart is the key
噢，我的策展人，妳的心就是那把鑰匙

為我翻譯這些石頭的沉默

它是這世界上唯一真實的事物
You are the pulse, the living book of old
妳是脈搏，是那本活著的古老史書 
Turning the grey dust into liquid gold
將灰色的塵土，煉成流動的金箔

The Sun God watches from the temple high
