Hēnōsis

Back in the cabin, salt in the breeze
回到小屋，微風中帶著鹽味
Two stones tied in a silver loop
兩顆石頭繫在銀環中
The Sun-Tying stone, the Life stone’s ease
栓日石與生命石的安適
Weaved together in a quiet scoop
在寧靜的懷抱中交織
The dust of Peru, now a memory’s light
秘魯的塵埃，如今成了記憶之光
Resting on the windowsill, through the night
整夜靜候在窗台上

Oh, Henosis, the knot we’ve tied
喔，萬物合一，這是我們繫下的結
Between the wild wind and the place we hide
在狂風與我們的避風港之間
You are the lighthouse, I am the shore
妳是燈塔，我是彼岸
No need to wander, no need for more
無需再流浪，無需再求索
Freedom and home, in a single thread
自由與歸宿，盡在一線之間
All the words of the past are finally said
過去的所有話語，終於都已說盡

Amber eyes, catching the dawn
琥珀色的眼眸，捕捉著黎明
You said I put the sun in your sight
妳說我讓太陽映入妳的眼簾
The shadows of brothers, the ghosts are gone
過往的陰影，那些鬼魂都已散去
Healed by the fire, in the softest light
在最溫柔的光裡，被爐火治癒
A scavenger once, now a creator of us
曾是拾荒者，如今是我們生活的創造者
In this wooden silence, there is only trust
在這木屋的寂靜中，唯有信任

Let the silence be our sacred shield
讓寂靜成為我們神聖的盾牌
Let the scabs turn to skin, as the wounds are healed
讓結痂長回皮膚，隨傷口痊癒
The world is noisy, but the house is still
世界喧囂嘈雜，但屋內一片靜謐
We’ll spend the time, as only lovers will
我們將消磨時光，一如所有戀人那樣

Solo-via, Henora, mika-te sen
Lio-na, Sapa-ka, sun-ya-ren
Et-no-sa, ti-mar-o, kalla-waya
Oikos, Nan-yan, Xin-gui-ra e

The next destination is always home
下一個目的地永遠是家
Together we drift, together we roam
我們一同漂泊，我們一同漫遊
