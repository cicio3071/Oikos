In the Valley of Witness

The mist remembers what our hands have traced
白霧記得，那些手指描過的痕跡
a valley older than the names we gave
這溪谷比我們所有的名字都古老
the stones don't ask—they simply hold the weight
石頭不問，只是承接所有的重量
of two who came to listen, not to claim
兩個人來此聆聽，而非佔領

And I was taught to fight the ground beneath
而我曾被教導，要對抗腳下的土地
until your hand said — let it carry you
直到你的手說——讓它承載你

Open like the orchid on the stone
像石斛花，開在岩壁上
no one planted us, we simply grew
從未有人播種我們，我們只是生長了
held by what we cannot name or own
被那些無法命名、無法擁有的事物所托住
the valley breathes—and we breathe too
溪谷呼吸，而我們也呼吸
the valley breathes—and we breathe too
溪谷呼吸，而我們也呼吸

You learned to walk where water sets the pace
妳學會了跟著水流的節奏行走
to lean into the mountain, not to fight
向著山的方向傾倒，而非對抗
some roots grow deepest in the darkest place
有些根，在最深的暗處，長得最深
some things are only seen by letting go of sight
有些事物，只有在放棄凝視時才得以顯現

I stopped explaining what I felt inside
我停止解釋內心的感受
and pressed my face against your living warmth
將臉埋進你活生生的溫熱裡

Something older than desire
某種比慾望更古老的事物
watched us fall into each other's shape
看著我們，墜入彼此的輪廓
not a taking — a surrendering
不是奪取，是交付
written in the bark
刻在樹皮裡
inscribed in rain
書寫在雨裡
and we became the place
而我們，成為了那個地方
